Illustrative single-axis inertial-navigation error budget evaluated with the analytic model of \cite{r43_Narducci2022} (Schuler period 84.4~min \cite{r45_Cheiney2018}). (a)~Contributions of a 30~$\mu g$ accelerometer bias and a 0.05~$\mu$rad/s gyroscope bias, the aviation-grade values assumed in the simulation study of \cite{r44_Wright2022}. (b)~Total error for the classical case (S1); with the accelerometer bias replaced by the quantum-tracked residual of \cite{r45_Cheiney2018} (S2, laboratory with simulated mobile disturbances) or by the bias stability of \cite{r20_Templier2022} (S3, static laboratory; overlaps S2); and with the gyroscope bias set to the rotation stability of \cite{r46_Dutta2016} (S4, laboratory, not a navigation test). Dashed lines: end-of-trajectory map-matching errors from airborne and ground trials reported in a preprint \cite{r50_Muradoglu2025}; these are not time-resolved. Alignment errors, sensor noise and misalignment are not modeled.
