Detected squeezing versus detection efficiency for a pure-loss model, $V_\mathrm{obs}=\eta V_\mathrm{src}+(1-\eta)$, for several source levels; the black curve is the ceiling for infinite source squeezing. Open circle: Advanced LIGO budget, with total efficiency 0.75 and a predicted 4.1~dB from 7.2~dB generated at the source; the pure-loss model reproduces this prediction \cite{r03_Tse2019}. Filled circle: the measured 3.2~dB, for which \cite{r03_Tse2019} infers an efficiency of 0.66 from the same measurement, so agreement there is a consistency check rather than an independent test; the authors attribute the shortfall most likely to higher-than-estimated mode-matching and misalignment loss, and their estimated 15~mrad of phase noise lowers the prediction by only about 0.1~dB. Square: entangled optomechanical probes, with about 4~dB generated, 74\% detection efficiency and about 2.4~dB observed, reported as consistent with the overall loss \cite{r17_Xia2023}. Phase noise and anti-squeezing are not modeled. The arrow links budget to measurement; error bars are those of \cite{r03_Tse2019} (smaller than the symbol where not visible).
