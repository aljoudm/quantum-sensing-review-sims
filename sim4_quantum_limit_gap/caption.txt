(b)~Ratio of operating noise to a reference limit for three quantum sensors; the dashed line marks operation at the limit. Gravity gradiometer: 466~E/$\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}$ in an outdoor field test, relative to an estimated quantum projection noise of 44~E/$\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}$ \cite{r19_Stray2022} (evidence: field test). Nitrogen-vacancy (NV) magnetocardiography (MCG): 140~pT/$\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}$ in vivo in a shielded room, relative to a shot-noise limit of 19~pT/$\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}$ \cite{r22_Arai2022} (evidence: in vivo laboratory measurement). Rydberg electrometer (*, light gray): best reported value, about one order of magnitude above the antenna thermal limit \cite{r04_Kitching2025} (evidence: compiled review, second-hand). Note that the third bar is referenced to a classical thermal limit rather than a quantum limit, so it is not directly comparable with the other two.
