Maturity ranking of five application domains, based on the 52-source corpus. The primary ranking is the unweighted sum of five scores from 0 to 3 (maximum 15; Table~\ref{tab:loo}): technology readiness level (TRL), field demonstrations, comparison with incumbents, independent groups and commercial availability. The TRL score is not rated by the authors; it follows from the lower bound of the TRL range in Table VI of \cite{r06_Kantsepolsky2026} (TRL 1--3: 0; 4: 1; 5--6: 2; 7 or higher: 3). The other four are consensus scores agreed by the two authors. The figure shows a robustness analysis around the primary ranking: $N = 10000$ Monte Carlo draws in which the five criterion weights are sampled from a flat Dirichlet prior and each of the 20 consensus scores is, with probability 0.2, changed by $+1$ or $-1$ with equal chance and clipped to the range 0--3; the TRL score is held fixed. Left: probability of ranking first, counting every tie for first (so the values can sum to more than one). Right: median rank (dot) and 5th to 95th percentile range (line), with tied domains sharing the average rank; rank 1 is the most mature. The further robustness analyses are leave-one-criterion-out, a rerun with the upper TRL bound, an adversarial test of one-point score changes, and comparisons with the ranking implied by the TRL ranges of \cite{r06_Kantsepolsky2026}; the comparison that excludes TRL as a criterion (four judged criteria only) gives a Spearman rank correlation of \textbf{[SPEARMAN]}. Reference \cite{r50_Muradoglu2025} is a preprint; the figure includes it, and Table~\ref{tab:loo} also gives the primary sums without it. In the independent first rating round, agreement between the two authors on the four judged criteria was \textbf{[PERCENT-AGREEMENT]}\% exact, with a quadratic-weighted Cohen's $\kappa$ of \textbf{[KAPPA]} (95\% percentile bootstrap interval \textbf{[KAPPA-CI-LOW]} to \textbf{[KAPPA-CI-HIGH]}; 10000 resamples of the 20 judged pairs).
